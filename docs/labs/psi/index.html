PSI01 · PSI · Announced October 23, 2026 Three copies of the Werner state: the sharp curve in dimensions three and four and a bound in every dimension Open Quantum Problems 2: Undistillability implies PPT? PSI05 · PSI · Announced October 23, 2026 An extensibly causal quantum process with no purification Open Quantum Problems 43: Are all extensibly causal processes purifiable? By David Turturean, Ning Bao. PSI06 · PSI · Announced November 6, 2026 The Aaronson–Ambainis conjecture, proved? PSI’s AI proof in Lean, not yet checked outside PSI says a non-public Anthropic model found a proof of the Aaronson–Ambainis conjecture (open since 2008): every bounded polynomial on the cube of degree d with variance at least ε has a bit of influence at least (ε/d)7. A 60-page paper by David Turturean and a Lean proof of the influence bound; the recorded checks were PSI’s own (rented machines, private repositories). Not on arXiv, not peer reviewed; the quantum consequences are not in Lean. Anthropic makes Claude, which helps make this series.
