Result 097 · Convex and metric geometry

The Euclidean Steinitz–Bergström bound

OpenAI’s summary

Proves that any finite sequence in the Euclidean unit ball of ℝd admits signs keeping every partial sum within CdC\sqrt dCd​, independently of length. Consequently every zero-sum family can be reordered with the same bound on unsigned partial sums. A matching lower bound gives the optimal order S2(d)=Θ(d)S_2(d)=\Theta(\sqrt d)S2​(d)=Θ(d​).
