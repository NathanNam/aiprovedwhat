Result 258 · Group theory

Gersten’s conjecture and virtual compact specialness of one-relator groups

OpenAI’s summary

Proves Gersten's conjecture: every finitely generated one-relator group containing no Baumslag–Solitar subgroup BS(m,n)\mathrm{BS}(m,n)BS(m,n), with m,n≠0m,n\ne0m,n=0, is word-hyperbolic. It also proves that every word-hyperbolic one-relator group is virtually compact special.
