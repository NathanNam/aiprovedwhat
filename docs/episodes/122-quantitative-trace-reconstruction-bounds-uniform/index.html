Result 122 · Theoretical computer science

Quantitative trace-reconstruction bounds with a uniform decoder

OpenAI’s summary

At every fixed deletion probability in (0,1)(0,1)(0,1), reconstructing an arbitrary length-n binary string requires nΩ(log⁡log⁡n)n^{\Omega(\log\log n)}nΩ(loglogn) independent traces, ruling out polynomial-sample reconstruction. A uniform decoder achieves quasipolynomial sample and running-time bounds for known fixed rational retention probabilities. When the deletion probability is at most n−εn^{-\varepsilon}n−ε for fixed ε > 0, both bounds become polynomial in the input and parameter encoding.
