Result 379 · Theoretical computer science

Separation of VP and VNP over every field

OpenAI’s summary

An explicit VNP family requires border arithmetic circuits of size at least exp⁡(Ω((log⁡n)5/4))\exp(\Omega((\log n)^{5/4}))exp(Ω((logn)5/4)) over every field. The same bound holds for the directed Hamiltonian cycle polynomial over every field and for the permanent outside characteristic two.
