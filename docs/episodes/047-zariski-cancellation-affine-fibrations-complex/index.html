Result 047 · Algebraic and complex geometry

Zariski cancellation and affine fibrations over the complex numbers

OpenAI’s summary

Constructs an integral complex affine fourfold X≇A4X\not\cong\mathbb A^4X≅A4 with X×A1≅A5X\times\mathbb A^1\cong\mathbb A^5X×A1≅A5, disproving affine-space cancellation over ℂ in dimension four. It also disproves the Dolgachev–Weisfeiler affine-fibration conjecture: smooth surjections X→A1X\to\mathbb A^1X→A1 and A5→A2\mathbb A^5\to\mathbb A^2A5→A2 have every residue-field fiber isomorphic to affine three-space but are not Zariski-locally trivial.
