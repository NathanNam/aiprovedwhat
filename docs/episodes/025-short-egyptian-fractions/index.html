Short Egyptian fractions

OpenAI’s summary

Every rational a/ba/ba/b with 1≤a<b1\le a\lt b1≤a<b is a sum of O(log⁡log⁡b)O(\log\log b)O(loglogb) distinct positive unit fractions. The worst-case minimum number of terms has the same order, resolving Erdős’s conjecture on short Egyptian fractions.
