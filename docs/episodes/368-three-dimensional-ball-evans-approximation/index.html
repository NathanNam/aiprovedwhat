The three-dimensional Ball–Evans approximation problem

OpenAI’s summary

Resolves the three-dimensional Ball–Evans approximation problem: every W1,pW^{1,p}W1,p homeomorphism between arbitrary bounded domains in ℝ3, for 1≤p<∞1\le p\lt \infty1≤p<∞, is a strong W1,pW^{1,p}W1,p limit of smooth diffeomorphisms onto the same target.
