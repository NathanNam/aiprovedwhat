Result 377 · Partial differential equations

Interior C1,αC^{1,\alpha}C1,α regularity for infinity-harmonic functions

OpenAI’s summary

Proves uniform interior C1,αdC^{1,\alpha_d}C1,αd​ regularity for bounded infinity-harmonic functions in every dimension d ≥ 3, for a positive exponent depending only on dimension. The gradient's supremum norm and Hölder seminorm on the half unit ball are bounded by a dimension-dependent constant times the oscillation on the unit ball. The exponent is not explicit.
