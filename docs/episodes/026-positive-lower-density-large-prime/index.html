Result 026 · Number theory

Positive lower density of large prime gaps

OpenAI’s summary

For every fixed C > 0, a positive proportion of consecutive prime gaps exceed Clog⁡pnC\log p_nClogpn​, throughout every sufficiently large initial segment of the primes. The proportion may depend on C. Consequently, the indices where pn/np_n/npn​/n increases have positive lower density, answering Erdős and Prachar.
