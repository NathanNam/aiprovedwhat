The claim, in plain words

A vector bundle is nef if its tautological line bundle has nonnegative degree on every curve. Rational homogeneous varieties, the quotients G/P of a semisimple group by a parabolic subgroup, include projective spaces, quadrics, Grassmannians and flag varieties. The group’s vector fields span every tangent space, so their tangent bundles are generated by global sections and are nef. Mori showed that an ample tangent bundle forces projective space. In 1991 Campana and Peternell conjectured the analogue for nef: every Fano manifold with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous.

They proved it up to dimension three and for fourfolds of higher Picard number. Mok and Hwang finished the fourfolds using minimal rational curves, and Watanabe and Kanemitsu finished the fivefolds. Kanemitsu also proved it whenever the Picard number exceeds the dimension minus five. (A survey describes the state of the art in 2014.) For sixfolds that leaves Picard number one, and standard characterizations reduce to pseudoindex four or five, where the homogeneous examples are the Lagrangian Grassmannian LG(3,6) and the Grassmannian Gr(2,5).

Result 067 is one OpenAI preprint (27 pages, dated September 25, 2026). It claims the conjecture in dimension six: every smooth connected complex projective Fano sixfold with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous. Pseudoindex four had been treated by Watanabe; the paper gives an independent argument. The new step is pseudoindex five. With the structure theorem of Demailly, Peternell and Schneider, it also claims: a compact Kähler manifold with nef tangent bundle whose dimension minus its maximal irregularity is at most six has a finite cover whose Albanese map is a bundle with rational homogeneous fibers, and its universal cover is such a fiber times ℂq̃. In particular this holds in every dimension up to six.

What it doesn’t claim

Nothing about dimension seven or higher. The Kähler statement is not a separate proof: it follows from the sixfold theorem and the structure theorem of Demailly, Peternell and Schneider, and it needs the dimension minus the maximal irregularity to be at most six.

How the proof goes

  1. Minimal rational curves. For pseudoindex five, the rational curves of least anticanonical degree through a general point x form a smooth three-dimensional family F. Sending each curve to its tangent direction maps F to P(TxX) ≅ P⁵; the image is the variety of minimal rational tangents. For Gr(2,5) it is the Segre threefold P¹ × P².
  2. Two readings of one family. The universal family of the curves maps smoothly to X and to the space of curves. Read as a family of curves and as a family of threefolds over X, with Deligne’s fixed-part theorem and Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch, it yields linear and product identities among intersection numbers.
  3. One quadratic. A finite rational elimination, with an exact certificate, leaves 32 − 20u + 3u² = 0, where u = c₁(TF)·L²/L³ and L is the hyperplane class pulled back to F. So u is 4 or 8/3.
  4. The Segre threefold. Hwang and Mok’s results force the image to span P⁵. The sectional genus 1 + L³(2 − u)/2 cannot be negative: u = 4 would give a linear three-space, which cannot span P⁵, and u = 8/3 gives L³ = 3 and genus 0. A threefold of degree three spanning P⁵ is a scroll of minimal degree, and smoothness of F leaves only the Segre threefold. Mok’s recognition theorem then identifies X with Gr(2,5).
  5. Pseudoindex four. Kanemitsu’s factorization, Hard Lefschetz and intersection theory rule out the possible fibers; the last case, an odd-dimensional quadric bundle, would give an integral divisor a non-integral degree.

1:14The claim

Result 067 is one OpenAI preprint, 27 pages, dated September 25. It claims the conjecture in dimension six: every smooth complex Fano sixfold with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous. Pseudoindex four was treated by Watanabe; the paper gives an independent intersection argument for it. The new step is pseudoindex five, where the answer is the Grassmannian of two-planes in five-space. A consequence reaches beyond Fano manifolds. With the structure theorem of Demailly, Peternell and Schneider, a compact Kähler manifold with nef tangent bundle, whose dimension minus its maximal irregularity is at most six, has a finite cover whose Albanese map is a bundle with rational homogeneous fibers, and its universal cover is such a fiber times complex affine space. In particular this holds for every such manifold of dimension at most six.

2:11Minimal rational curves

For pseudoindex five, look at the rational curves of least anticanonical degree. The minimal curves through a general point x make up a smooth three-dimensional family F, and sending each curve to its tangent direction maps F to the projectivized tangent space, a P⁵. The image is the variety of minimal rational tangents. For the Grassmannian, it is the Segre threefold, P¹ × P², inside P⁵. The question is how to force that answer. The universal family of these curves maps to X and to the space of curves, and both maps are smooth. The paper uses it in two ways: as a family of curves, and as a family of threefolds over X. With Deligne’s fixed-part theorem and Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch, this gives linear identities and product identities among intersection numbers. A finite rational elimination, with an exact certificate, leaves one quadratic: 32 − 20u + 3u² = 0, where u is the first Chern class of F times L², divided by L³, and L is the hyperplane class pulled back to F.

3:20Pinning down the Grassmannian

So u is four or 8/3. Results of Hwang and Mok force the image to span P⁵. The sectional genus, 1 + L³(2 − u)/2, cannot be negative. For u equal to four, that forces L³ equal to one, a linear three-space, which cannot span P⁵. For 8/3, it forces L³ equal to three and genus zero. Degree three and spanning P⁵ make the image a scroll, and smoothness of F leaves only the Segre threefold, where L³ is three, the Chern number eight, and u 8/3. With the variety of minimal rational tangents identified as the Segre threefold, Mok’s recognition theorem identifies X with the Grassmannian of two-planes in five-space. For pseudoindex four, the paper uses Kanemitsu’s factorization, Hard Lefschetz and intersection theory to rule out the possible fibers. The last case is an odd-dimensional quadric bundle, where the second fibration would give an integral divisor a non-integral degree.

4:24Where it stands

The preprint is not yet peer reviewed, and it has not been revised. There is no Lean formalization, and it cites no other paper from the release. It ships a Python certificate for the finite elimination; we ran it, and it passed. Its inputs include Kanemitsu’s theorems, the theory of minimal rational curves of Hwang and Mok, Mok’s recognition theorem and Deligne’s fixed-part theorem. We found no public assessment. If it holds, every Fano sixfold with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous, like projective space, a quadric, a Grassmannian, a flag variety, or a product of these, and the Campana–Peternell conjecture is known through dimension six.
