Result 033 · Algebraic and complex geometry

Iitaka subadditivity, variation, and logarithmic additivity

OpenAI’s summary

Proves Campana's orbifold Iitaka subadditivity conjecture for smooth Fujiki-class-C\mathcal CC manifolds with rational simple-normal-crossing boundaries. For projective fibrations f:U→Vf:U\to Vf:U→V of smooth complex quasi-projective varieties with connected fibers, general fiber F, and κˉ(V)≥0\bar\kappa(V)\ge0κˉ(V)≥0, proves Popa's inequality κˉ(U)≥κ(F)+max⁡{κˉ(V),Var(f)}\bar\kappa(U)\ge\kappa(F)+\max\{\bar\kappa(V),\mathop{\mathrm{Var}}\nolimits (f)\}κˉ(U)≥κ(F)+max{κˉ(V),Var(f)}, where variation measures the whole geometric generic fiber.
