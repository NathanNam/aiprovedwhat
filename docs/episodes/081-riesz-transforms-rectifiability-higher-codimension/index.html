Result 081 · Real and complex analysis

Riesz transforms and rectifiability in higher codimension

OpenAI’s summary

Resolves the remaining higher-codimension Riesz-transform rectifiability problem: for d ≥ 4 and 2≤n≤d−22\le n\le d-22≤n≤d−2, an n-Ahlfors–David regular Radon measure on ℝd is uniformly n-rectifiable whenever its n-dimensional Riesz transform is uniformly L2-bounded over all positive hard truncations. The rectifiability bounds depend only on dimension, regularity and operator bounds.
