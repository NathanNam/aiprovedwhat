Result 082 · Real and complex analysis

Annular variation and dyadic absolute bounds for the triangular Hilbert transform

OpenAI’s summary

Proves maximal and annular r-variation bounds, for every r > 2, from complex L3(R2)×L3(R2)L^3(\mathbb R^2)\times L^3(\mathbb R^2)L3(R2)×L3(R2) to L3/2(R2)L^{3/2}(\mathbb R^2)L3/2(R2). The maximal estimate controls both hard truncation endpoints and gives almost-everywhere and norm convergence. Pairing with a third input settles the triangular Hilbert transform estimate at the symmetric L3×L3×L3L^3\times L^3\times L^3L3×L3×L3 point.
