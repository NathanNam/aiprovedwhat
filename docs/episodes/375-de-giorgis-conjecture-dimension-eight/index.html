Result 375 · Partial differential equations

De Giorgi's conjecture in dimension eight

OpenAI’s summary

Proves De Giorgi's conjecture at its sharp dimension-eight endpoint: every entire C2 solution u:R8→(−1,1)u:\mathbb R^8\to(-1,1)u:R8→(−1,1) of Δu=u3−u\Delta u=u^3-uΔu=u3−u that is strictly increasing in one direction depends on only one linear coordinate. A stronger theorem classifies all stable entire solutions v:R7→[−1,1]v:\mathbb R^7\to[-1,1]v:R7→[−1,1] as constant wells or planar transitions, without an energy-growth assumption.
