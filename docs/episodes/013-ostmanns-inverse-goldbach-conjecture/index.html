Result 013 · Number theory

Ostmann’s inverse Goldbach conjecture

OpenAI’s summary

Proves that no finite modification of the primes can be written as A+BA+BA+B with A,B⊆Z≥0A,B\subseteq\mathbb Z_{\ge0}A,B⊆Z≥0​ each containing at least two elements. This resolves Ostmann's inverse Goldbach conjecture on additive indecomposability.
