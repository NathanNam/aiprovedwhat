Perfect completeness for 2-to-1 games

OpenAI’s summary

Proves Khot's 2-to-1 Games Conjecture with perfect completeness: for every fixed rational δ∈(0,1)\delta\in(0,1)δ∈(0,1), it is NP-hard to distinguish satisfiable games from games whose optimum is at most δ, on explicit unweighted instances. The alphabet depends only on δ, and every right-hand label has exactly two preimages under each constraint map.
