Result 277 · Mathematical physics

Threshold repetition for entangled games

OpenAI’s summary

Proves exponential threshold repetition for every finite two-player one-round game: if its entangled value is v < 1, the probability of winning at least a fraction v+δv+\deltav+δ of k independent repetitions decays exponentially in k, for 0<δ<1−v0\lt \delta\lt 1-v0<δ<1−v. Arbitrary joint finite-dimensional entangled strategies and correlated question distributions are allowed.
