Result 037 · Algebraic and complex geometry

The ordinary-double-point volume gap

OpenAI’s summary

Proves the ordinary-double-point volume-gap conjecture: every singular complex algebraic klt germ of dimension n ≥ 2, with zero boundary, has normalized volume at most 2(n−1)n2(n-1)^n2(n−1)n. Equality holds precisely for an analytic ordinary double point.
