Result 232 · Probability and statistical mechanics

Gaussian fields and interfaces for triangular-lattice Lipschitz heights

OpenAI’s summary

Proves Gaussian free field limits on bounded smooth simply connected domains for triangular-lattice height models: uniform odd heights with increments 0,±20,\pm20,±2 and two-arc boundary values ±1\pm1±1, and zero-boundary integer Lipschitz heights weighted by fixed x∈[1/2,1]x\in[1/\sqrt2,1]x∈[1/2​,1]. Uniform real Lipschitz heights also converge to a Gaussian field; at a tuned opposite-boundary amplitude, their interface converges to chordal SLE4, establishing Schramm’s real-field/interface predictions.
