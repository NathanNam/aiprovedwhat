Result 075 · Real and complex analysis

The Llog⁡LL\log LLlogL Fourier-convergence conjecture

OpenAI’s summary

Proves that the ordinary symmetric Fourier partial sums of every complex-valued function in Llog⁡L(T)L\log L(\mathbb T)LlogL(T) converge almost everywhere along the full sequence. This resolves the classical sufficiency conjecture at the Llog⁡LL\log LLlogL scale.
