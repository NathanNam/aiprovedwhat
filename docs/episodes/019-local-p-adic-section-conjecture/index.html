Result 019 · Number theory

The local p-adic section conjecture and global consequences

OpenAI’s summary

Proves that rational points on every smooth proper geometrically connected curve of genus at least two over a finite extension of ℚp correspond bijectively to conjugacy classes of sections of its full arithmetic étale fundamental group. It also proves Grothendieck's section conjecture over ℚ for the modular curves X0(N)X_0(N)X0​(N) and X1(N)X_1(N)X1​(N) of genus at least two.
