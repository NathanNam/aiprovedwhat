Result 207 · Algebra

The ℓ¹-Bass conjecture for all discrete groups

OpenAI’s summary

Proves the ℓ1-Bass conjecture for every discrete group: Hattori–Stallings traces of idempotent matrices over ℓ1(G)\ell^1(G)ℓ1(G) are supported on finitely many finite-order conjugacy classes. The algebraic companion proves the integral Bass trace conjecture and Kaplansky's idempotent conjecture for torsion-free groups over every commutative unital characteristic-zero domain.
