Result 266 · Mathematical physics

Exactly three mutually unbiased bases in dimension six

OpenAI’s summary

Proves N(6)=3N(6)=3N(6)=3, resolving Zauner's dimension-six mutually unbiased bases conjecture: three such bases exist in ℂ6, but four cannot. The exclusion is a complete certified computation under the stated binary64 arithmetic and compiler conditions. An independent companion proves the Matolcsi–Ruzsa–Weiner Fourier-vanishing conjecture for order-six complex Hadamard matrices outside Tao's cubic equivalence class.
