Result 181 · Combinatorics

The Erdős–Gallai cycle-decomposition conjecture

OpenAI’s summary

Proves that the edges of every finite simple undirected graph on n vertices can be partitioned into at most CnCnCn simple cycles and single edges, for an absolute constant C. This resolves the Erdős–Gallai cycle-decomposition conjecture, bounding the number of pieces linearly even for dense graphs.
