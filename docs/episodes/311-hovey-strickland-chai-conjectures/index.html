Result 311 · Topology

The Hovey–Strickland and Chai conjectures

OpenAI’s summary

Proves Chai's invariant-ideal conjecture for Lubin–Tate deformation rings over finite residue fields, at every prime and positive height n. Through the implication of Barthel–Heard–Naumann, this proves the Hovey–Strickland conjecture: dualizable K(n)K(n)K(n)-local spectra have exactly n+2n+2n+2 thick tensor ideals, and their Balmer spectrum is a chain of n+1n+1n+1 points.
