Result 016 · Number theory

Zilber–Pink in abelian varieties and the Siegel threefold

OpenAI’s summary

Proves the abelian Zilber–Pink conjecture over Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​: every irreducible subvariety has finitely many maximal atypical subvarieties relative to its smallest containing torsion coset. It also proves the full curve case in the Siegel threefold A2\mathcal A_2A2​ for Hodge-generic curves defined over Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​, without boundary or reduction assumptions.
