Result 092 · Convex and metric geometry

The optimal order of convex-body covering density

OpenAI’s summary

Determines the optimal worst-case covering density as Θ(nlog⁡n)\Theta(n\log n)Θ(nlogn), for both lattice and unrestricted translative coverings. Every convex body in ℝn, n ≥ 2, admits a lattice covering of density at most Cnlog⁡nCn\log nCnlogn; centrally symmetric examples in every sufficiently large dimension require at least cnlog⁡ncn\log ncnlogn even without the lattice restriction, for absolute c,C>0c,C\gt 0c,C>0.
