Result 208 · Algebra

Finite symmetric tensor categories and the Verlinde tower

OpenAI’s summary

Proves that every finite symmetric tensor category over an algebraically closed field k of characteristic p > 0 admits a k-linear exact faithful strong symmetric monoidal fiber functor to a higher Verlinde category Verpn\mathrm{Ver}_{p^n}Verpn​. The level may depend on the category, and the theorem includes characteristic two, resolving the finite case of the Benson–Etingof–Ostrik conjecture.
