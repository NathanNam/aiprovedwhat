The claim, in plain words

Two-dimensional conformal field theory describes systems at a critical point, like the Ising magnet at its phase transition. Mathematicians have two rigorous languages for its chiral half. A vertex operator algebra describes the fields through their algebra, an infinite family of modes. A conformal net assigns to every arc of a circle an algebra of bounded observables, and observables on disjoint arcs commute. In 2015, Carpi, Kawahigashi, Longo and Weiner built a bridge: a unitary vertex operator algebra that is strongly local generates a conformal net. They conjectured that every simple unitary one is strongly local, and proved it for affine algebras, the Virasoro algebra and the moonshine module; since then more families have followed, lattices among them.

Result 280 is one OpenAI preprint, 47 pages, dated September 25, 2026. It claims the strongly rational case of the conjecture: every simple unitary vertex operator algebra that is rational, C2-cofinite, of CFT type and self-dual has polynomial energy bounds, is strongly local, and generates a completely rational conformal net. It also claims that every simple module is unitarizable, and that Huang’s modular tensor category is equivalent, as a braided unitary tensor category, to the net’s finite-index sectors. For the Ising model, the spin field has quantum dimension √2 and the net’s μ-index is 4, the sum of the squared dimensions.

What it doesn’t claim

The general conjecture, for algebras that are not strongly rational; the reverse direction, from nets back to vertex operator algebras; or any classification. Many examples were already known. What is new is one proof for the whole class.

How the proof goes

  1. A speed limit for fields. Fields are unbounded operators, and fields that commute on paper need not give commuting observables. The fix is an energy bound: each mode must be bounded by a power of the energy, with a constant growing only polynomially in the mode number. The paper gets a crude bound and feeds it back into itself, first to a polynomial bound and then to the optimal one, and a criterion of Carpi, Tanimoto and Weiner then gives strong locality.
  2. Positive modules, matching categories. To give a module a positive inner product, the proof doubles the theory and finds the module, paired with its dual, inside a fusion product of pieces already known to be positive. Gui’s comparison theory then matches fusion and braiding with the net’s Connes fusion and braiding, and, using reconstruction work of Henriques and Tener, the paper shows the net has no extra sectors.

The hard part is analysis. Fields are unbounded operators, and fields that commute on paper need not give commuting observables. For a free boson, a toy example, our computer finds the creation modes grow like √n. Next, every module needs a positive inner product.
