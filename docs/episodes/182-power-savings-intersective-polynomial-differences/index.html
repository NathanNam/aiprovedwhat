Power savings for intersective polynomial differences and prime arguments

OpenAI’s summary

For every fixed intersective integer polynomial h of degree k ≥ 2 with positive leading coefficient, proves that a subset of {1,…,N}\{1,\ldots,N\}{1,…,N} avoiding nonzero values h(1),h(2),…h(1),h(2),\ldotsh(1),h(2),… as differences has size Oh(N1−ck)O_h(N^{1-c_k})Oh​(N1−ck​), with ck>0c_k\gt 0ck​>0 depending only on degree. Here intersective means having a root modulo every modulus. For prime arguments, a power saving also holds when h has a unit root modulo every modulus, with exponent allowed to depend on h.
