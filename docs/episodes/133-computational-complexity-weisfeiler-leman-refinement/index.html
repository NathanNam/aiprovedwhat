Result 133 · Theoretical computer science

The computational complexity of Weisfeiler–Leman refinement

OpenAI’s summary

Proves unconditional nΩ(k)n^{\Omega(k)}nΩ(k) deterministic time lower bounds for joint and separate k-dimensional Weisfeiler–Leman equivalence, for sufficiently large fixed k in the specified sequential adjacency-matrix models. With dimension as input, joint equivalence is EXPTIME-complete even on subcubic graphs; deciding whether refinement identifies a graph is also EXPTIME-complete.
