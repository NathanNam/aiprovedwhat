Result 011 · Number theory

Prime-factor statistics of p−1p-1p−1

OpenAI’s summary

Proves that the normalized ordered logarithms of the prime factors of p−1p-1p−1, counted with multiplicity, converge jointly to the Poisson–Dirichlet law PD(1)\mathrm{PD}(1)PD(1) as p ranges uniformly over primes up to x and x→∞x\to\inftyx→∞. This resolves the Ford–Konyagin–Luca conjecture. It also proves that infinitely many integers n have more than n1−εn^{1-\varepsilon}n1−ε totient preimages, for every ε > 0.
