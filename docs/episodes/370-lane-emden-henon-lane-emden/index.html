The Lane–Emden and Hénon–Lane–Emden conjectures

OpenAI’s summary

Resolves the subcritical Lane–Emden conjecture and its weighted Hénon extension. For n ≥ 2, p,q>0p,q\gt 0p,q>0 and real A, B, the system −Δu=∣x∣Avp-\Delta u=|x|^A v^p−Δu=∣x∣Avp, −Δv=∣x∣Buq-\Delta v=|x|^B u^q−Δv=∣x∣Buq has no positive entire solution when (n+A)/(p+1)+(n+B)/(q+1)>n−2(n+A)/(p+1)+(n+B)/(q+1)\gt n-2(n+A)/(p+1)+(n+B)/(q+1)>n−2, with solutions continuous at the origin and classical elsewhere. No symmetry or growth assumption is needed. Known radial existence gives the exact existence criterion for n ≥ 3 and A,B>−2A,B\gt -2A,B>−2.
