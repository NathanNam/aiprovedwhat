Result 142 · Theoretical computer science

Deterministic polynomial factorization over prime fields

OpenAI’s summary

Gives a uniform deterministic algorithm that completely factors every nonzero dense degree-n polynomial over a prime field 𝔽p, including multiplicities, in bit complexity polynomial in (n+1)log⁡p(n+1)\log p(n+1)logp. The prime is supplied in binary. No randomness, integer-factorization or primitive-root oracle, or GRH assumption is required.
