Result 238 · Probability and statistical mechanics

Optimal logarithmic mixing of the Thorp shuffle

OpenAI’s summary

Proves that the Thorp shuffle randomizes N=2dN=2^dN=2d labeled cards in Θ(log⁡N)\Theta(\log N)Θ(logN) physical shuffles, settling its optimal mixing order for power-of-two deck sizes. Convergence is in total variation from the worst initial ordering and concerns the entire permutation, not just individual card positions.
