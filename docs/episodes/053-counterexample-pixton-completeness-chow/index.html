The claim, in plain words

The moduli space Mg,n collects stable curves of genus g with n marked points, nodes allowed. Its tautological ring is spanned by psi and kappa classes pushed forward from boundary strata, the loci where the curve degenerates along a given dual graph. Describing it means knowing every relation among these generators. In 2012, Pixton proposed relations given as weighted sums over decorated dual graphs and conjectured that the span Pg,n they generate is the whole kernel of the map from formal graph sums to the Chow ring (and to cohomology). Pandharipande, Pixton and Zvonkine proved the relations hold in cohomology, and Janda proved them in Chow; completeness was open, and has been confirmed in some cases, recently including compact-type spaces studied by Canning, Larson and Schmitt.

Result 053 is an OpenAI preprint (dated September 24, 2026; 61 pages). It constructs an explicit formal combination of boundary strata Y whose value in the rational Chow ring is zero but which is not in Pixton’s original span, so Pg,n ⊊ ker q, in Chow and in rational cohomology. The example has genus 1060 and 3·C(g, 3) ≈ 5 × 10179 markings; the paper says these sizes are not meant to be minimal. Y is built by coloring the markings with g colors, using every triple of colors once: for each triple (a, b, c) the factor is a product of two sums of rational-tail divisors minus half a class supported on rational bridges, and Y is the product of all factors, alternated over relabelings that keep each color.

What it doesn’t claim

The comparison is with Pixton’s original relation span as the paper defines it, and the example lives in genus 1060; the paper gives no counterexample in small genus. It is also a different question from the Gorenstein property: earlier in September Pixton showed that the tautological ring of M25 is not Gorenstein, while also showing that the known relations are complete there.

How the proof goes

  1. A formal test. A linear map keeps only rational-tails graphs (one vertex of genus g, everything else rational tails) and evaluates the tails in a Frobenius algebra with unit, top class e and 2g odd symplectic vectors, whose handle element is (2 − 2g)e. Its coefficients lie in an exterior algebra with one odd symbol θijk per triple of colors, and three basic odd vectors multiply to θijke.
  2. Pixton’s relations pass, Y fails. Every element of Pixton’s span has test zero, while one coefficient of the test of Y is a nonzero multiple of the product of all the θ’s. So Y is outside the span.
  3. The cubic and its nilpotence. For a smooth curve, the small diagonal projected to three odd summands of its Chow correspondences gives an alternating cubic (related to the Gross–Schoen modified diagonal). A Quot scheme with fixed determinant on the surface C × C gives an identity making long enough products of these cubics vanish in Chow, up to errors supported over smaller parts of the family; a localization scalar is shown to be nonzero.
  4. The compactification. Weighted stable curves and a rational-bridge correction with coefficient −1/2 carry the identity to Mg,n, and a support induction, with enough colors at every step, removes the errors: Y is zero in rational Chow.

0:00Relations among natural classes

The moduli space M̄g,n collects all stable curves of genus g with n marked points, where nodes are allowed. It carries natural classes: psi classes from the cotangent lines at the markings, kappa classes, and classes of boundary strata, where the curve degenerates along a dual graph. Psi and kappa classes pushed forward from these strata span the tautological ring. To know that ring, you need every relation among these generators. In 2012, Pixton wrote down a uniform family of relations, built as weighted sums over decorated dual graphs, and conjectured that they, with everything they generate, are all the relations. Pandharipande, Pixton and Zvonkine proved that the relations hold in cohomology, and Janda proved them in the Chow ring. Completeness was the open part. It has been confirmed in some cases, recently including compact-type spaces studied by Canning, Larson and Schmitt.

0:58The claim

Result 053 is a 61-page OpenAI preprint, dated September 24. It constructs an explicit formal combination of boundary strata, Y, whose value in the rational Chow ring is zero, so it is a genuine relation, but which does not lie in the span of Pixton’s original relations. So Pixton’s completeness conjecture, for his original relations, would fail, in Chow and in rational cohomology. The example is enormous: genus 10^60, with 3·C(g, 3) markings. The paper says these sizes are not meant to be minimal. The class is built from boundary strata. Give the markings g colors, using every triple of colors once. For each triple, multiply two sums of rational-tail divisors, and subtract half of a class supported on rational bridges. Multiply all these factors, and alternate signs over relabelings that keep each color. The proof splits in two: a formal test showing Y is outside Pixton’s span, and geometry showing Y vanishes.

2:04A test the relations pass

The formal test sends each decorated graph to a value in an algebra. It keeps only graphs where one vertex carries all the genus and everything else is rational tails, and it evaluates the tails by multiplying and comultiplying in a Frobenius algebra, as in a two-dimensional topological field theory. The algebra has a unit, a top class e, and 2g odd vectors with a symplectic pairing, like the cohomology of a genus g curve. Its handle element is (2 − 2g)e. The twist is in the coefficients. They live in an exterior algebra with one independent odd symbol, theta, for every triple of colors, and multiplying three of the basic odd vectors produces the matching theta. The paper checks that every one of Pixton’s relations has test value zero. But one coefficient of the test of Y is a nonzero multiple of the product of all the thetas. So Y cannot be in Pixton’s span.

3:04Why Y vanishes

Actual geometry is more constrained. For a smooth curve, the small diagonal, projected to three odd pieces, gives an alternating cubic, related to the classical modified diagonal cycle. On the surface C times C, a Quot scheme with fixed determinant yields an extra identity: long enough products of these cubics vanish in Chow, up to error terms supported on smaller parts of the family. That is a nilpotence the formal test ignores, because there the thetas are independent and their product survives. The paper computes a localization scalar for this identity and shows it is nonzero, so the vanishing really holds. Then it passes to the compactification, using weighted stable curves and the correction on rational bridges with coefficient −1/2, and an induction that shrinks the support of the error until nothing is left. The huge genus leaves enough colors for every step. The conclusion: Y is zero in Chow.

4:03Where it stands

This is a preprint, not yet peer reviewed, and it has not been revised. The main theorem has no Lean formalization; the release’s Lean code checks only some bookkeeping, such as the color counts and that the product of all the thetas is nonzero. The paper cites no other paper from the release. Earlier in September, Pixton showed that the tautological ring of M₂₅ is not Gorenstein, while also showing that the known relations are complete there. That is a different question: Gorenstein is about pairings, completeness is about relations. We found no public assessment of this paper. If it holds, Pixton’s relations are not the whole story: some tautological relations come from geometry they don’t see, at least in very high genus.
