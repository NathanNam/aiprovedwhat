  • 155 A counterexample to periodic tiling in dimension three

    Constructs a finite translational tile in ℤ3 that tiles space but admits no fully periodic tiling, disproving the periodic tiling conjecture in the smallest possible lattice dimension. Its unit-cube thickening gives the same counterexample in ℝ3, even with arbitrary real translation vectors.

    OpenAI published a Lean proof of the main theorem.
