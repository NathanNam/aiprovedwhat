  8. October 26, 2026

    Result 079 extended to every dimension: a new paper, dated October 25, proves Sogge’s local smoothing conjecture for the wave equation in every spatial dimension n ≥ 2, with a Lean proof. OpenAI says it also yields the Kakeya maximal and dimension conjectures and Stein’s spherical restriction estimates. By our reading it implies the claims of 074 and 078 and the sphere and paraboloid cases of 077. Its new episode is out. Revised
