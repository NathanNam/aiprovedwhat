Updates

What changed in OpenAI’s math release and in the claims we follow: new results, revised and withdrawn papers, new Lean proofs, and claims announced outside the catalogue. Newest first, back to the first release.

Follow with RSS. Last updated October 26, 2026.

  1. October 26, 2026

    New result 378: OpenAI claims the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false, with a deterministic algorithm that counts satisfying assignments of k-CNF formulas in subexponential time and polynomial space, and a Lean proof. OpenAI says an independent working group of computer scientists led by Russell Impagliazzo, Ramamohan Paturi and Ryan Williams will examine and explain the proof. Episode out

    Source · See its connections

  2. October 26, 2026

    Result 079 extended to every dimension: a new paper, dated October 25, proves Sogge’s local smoothing conjecture for the wave equation in every spatial dimension n ≥ 2, with a Lean proof. OpenAI says it also yields the Kakeya maximal and dimension conjectures and Stein’s spherical restriction estimates. By our reading it implies the claims of 074 and 078 and the sphere and paraboloid cases of 077. A new episode is coming. Revised

    Source · See its connections

  3. October 26, 2026

    Lean proof added for 032, the rational Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties. Its scope notes leave out the consequences for the Tate and Hodge standard conjectures. Lean

    Source · See its connections

  4. October 26, 2026

    Code restored for 266: OpenAI restored the C++ source and saved numerical records for the computation that excludes a fourth mutually unbiased basis in dimension six, with a Python checker.

    Source · See its connections

  5. October 7, 2026

    Three papers withdrawn after a sign error in “Algebraicity of Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds” and two papers that depend on it; 14 other manuscripts revised and 6 Lean formalizations added. The catalogue now holds 719 manuscripts in 372 results. Withdrawn

    Source

  6. October 6, 2026

    OpenAI’s math release: github.com/openai/math opens with 722 manuscripts in 372 result families.
